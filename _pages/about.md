I am a Ph.D. student in Industrial and Systems Engineering at the **University of Minnesota Twin Cities**, advised by [Prof. Liyan Xie](https://cse.umn.edu/isye/liyan-xie). Since Summer 2026, I have also served as a **Graduate Research Assistant**.

---

### Research

My research develops statistical, coding-theoretic, and optimization methods for **trustworthy and robust learning systems**. I work on LLM watermarking and provenance, distributionally robust inference under model misspecification, and uncertainty-aware sequential modeling.

- **Watermark integrity** — detecting edits and localizing suspicious regions.
- **Watermarkability** — measuring and improving compatibility with logit-based watermarks.
- **Sequential and robust learning** — uncertainty-aware agentic simulation and inference for delayed point processes.

---
